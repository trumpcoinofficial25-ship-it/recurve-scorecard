Scope boundary

Arithmetic aid, not an official result

This checks typed values only. It cannot judge an arrow, correct an official card, confirm signatures, perform a shoot-off, or guarantee acceptance by an organizer. Local and event rules may differ.

Input format

One end per line; six values in descending order. Allowed: X, 10–1, M. A numeric 0 is rejected.

Ordinary long-distance ranking uses Xs first, then 10s. A cutoff tie in the rule’s stated cases requires an official shoot-off; X/10 counts do not settle it.

Pinned rule meaning

What this checker treats as known

X

Inner 10. It scores 10 points and is counted both among 10s and among Xs.

10

Scores 10 points. The displayed 10s count includes Xs, matching 10s+Xs scorecard usage.

M

Miss. It scores zero and must be written as M; this checker rejects a guessed “0” alias.

Order

Each end is entered from highest to lowest; X precedes a plain 10 for checking order.

Deliberate unknowns

What still belongs to judges and organizers

  • Whether a physical arrow is X, 10, another value, or M.
  • Corrections, signatures, duplicate-card precedence, sanctions, and official acceptance.
  • Shoot-off score, closest-to-centre measurement, and chart-position procedures.
  • Any local, national, para, age-class, match-play, compound, barebow, indoor, field, or 3D variant.
